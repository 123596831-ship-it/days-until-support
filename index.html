Frequently asked questions

How do I add an event?
Open the app and tap the plus button. Enter a name and date, then tap Save.

How do I show an event in the widget?
Open the event and turn on Pin to top. Add the Days Until widget to your Home Screen. If there are no events, the widget shows the countdown to the new year.

How do reminders work?
Turn on Remind me on the day in an event. Allow notifications when asked. On supported iOS versions, you can choose the system alarm sound in the event editor.

Privacy

Events are stored on your device and shared locally with the widget. The app does not require an account, collect personal data, or track you.
